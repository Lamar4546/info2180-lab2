Building My First Survival Base

September 27, 2026 by Lamar

When I started my new survival world, the first thing I did was punch a few trees and craft a workbench before the sun went down. Night comes quickly in Minecraft, and the first evening is always the most dangerous because you have almost nothing to defend yourself with. I dug into the side of a hill and sealed the entrance with dirt, which kept the zombies out until morning.

Over the next few days I expanded that tiny hole into a proper base with a farm, a storage room and a small mine shaft. I learned that planning your layout early saves a lot of time later. Labelling chests, keeping torches evenly spaced and always carrying a bed made the world feel much safer, and I finally had a place I was proud to call home.

Exploring the Nether for the First Time

October 3, 2026 by Lamar

After collecting enough obsidian, I lit my first Nether portal and stepped through with a nervous mix of excitement and fear. The red landscape, the lava lakes and the strange sounds made it feel like another universe. I stayed close to the portal at first, because ghasts can destroy a careless explorer in seconds.

Eventually I built a safe bridge across the lava and found a Nether fortress full of blaze spawners. Fighting blazes was tough, but the blaze rods were worth every heart I lost. My biggest tip is to bring plenty of cobblestone, a bucket of water and good armour, because the Nether does not forgive mistakes.
